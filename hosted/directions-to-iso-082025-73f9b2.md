The Baylor College of Medicine (BCM) International Services Office (ISO) is located at **One Baylor Plaza, Suite 672E (6th floor), Houston, TX 77030**. You will need to show a photo ID, such as a passport, driver's license, or BCM ID badge, to enter the building.

## Transportation options

- **Rideshare (Uber, Lyft, Alto):** Set the drop-off location to One Baylor Plaza, Houston, TX 77030.
- **Car / self-parking:** Park at TMC Garage 6. Visitor parking rates: tmc.edu/parking/visitor-parking.
- **BCM Shuttle:** Only available to those with a BCM badge. Track the shuttle at tmc.passiogo.com.
  - Green Shuttle: Get off at Bertner and Moursund.
  - Red Shuttle: Get off at John Freeman at E. Cullen.
  - Blue Shuttle: Get off at One Baylor Plaza - Fountain (BCM Main).
- **METRO (Bus or Train):** Use Google Maps for directions to One Baylor Plaza, Houston, TX 77030. METRO fare details: ridemetro.org/fares/all-about-fares.

## From the main entrance of One Baylor Plaza to ISO

1. Show photo ID at the security desk. If you are a new hire, mention it. Turn left after security.
2. Walk down the hallway until you reach the first T-shaped intersection. You will see Jaworski Classroom on your left. Turn right.
3. Continue straight. When you reach the next T-intersection, with elevators on your right, turn right again.
4. Walk to the end of the hallway. In the middle, it will curve slightly to the right. Keep going until the hallway ends.
5. Take the elevator to the 6th floor, turn right, and go to Suite 672E.

## From TMC Garage 6 to ISO

1. Exit the garage elevator on the 1st floor and enter the building. A blue ticket machine is available where you can pay for parking after your visit to ISO, when you are ready to exit the garage.
2. Show photo ID at the security desk. If you are a new hire, mention it. Go through the outdoor corridor.
3. Walk to the end of the outdoor corridor and re-enter the building. Turn left.
4. Walk to the end of the hallway. In the middle, it will curve slightly to the right. Keep going until the hallway ends.
5. Take the elevator to the 6th floor, turn right, and go to Suite 672E.
